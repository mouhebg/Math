Unit 01 // Session D // Extra practice 2

Rekenrek lab

What you need: A 20-bead rekenrek with two rows of 10 beads, plus a pencil. Use the real rack for every question.

See groups of 5 and 10, build numbers to 20, and add without counting every bead one by one.

Name:
Date:

Set up the rack

  1. Push every bead to the right. This is zero.
  2. Move beads to the left to show a number.
  3. See the group of 5 by its colour. See a full row as 10.
  4. For a number greater than 10, fill the top row, then use the bottom row.

An adult says each number. Build it on the rack, then write how you saw it.

Play: flash, cover, explain.

An adult builds one number, shows it briefly, then covers the rack with paper. Say the number and explain the groups you saw. There is no score.

Move on when

The student builds and reads numbers to 20 without counting every bead, describes a number as 5 and some more or 10 and some more, and solves the addition facts by making ten or using a double.

For the parent

If Donia begins counting beads one by one, reset the rack and ask only: What group can you see already? Follow with: Five and how many more? or Ten and how many more?

Use the rack for five to ten minutes at a time. Stop after a successful answer, then repeat questions 3 and 5 on another day.
