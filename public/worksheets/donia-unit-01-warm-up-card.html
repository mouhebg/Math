Unit 01 // For the parent // Two minutes

Before the sheet

Worksheets show what she already knows. Recall is built out loud, fast, with no pencil. Run this before Session A, then every day after it. Two minutes daily beats twenty minutes once a week.

00:00

Say the rules once, then start

"Ten questions, quick as you can. If you do not know one, just say pass. Pass costs nothing. We stop at two minutes." Then keep to it. If she stalls, say the answer yourself and move on.

Round 3, how did you know?

Pick one from round 2 and ask how she got it. This is the whole diagnostic. What she says tells you what to do next.

What you hearWhat it meansWhat to do
Answers instantly, no visible effortShe knows itMove on to Session B
Short pause, then correct, "I know 5 and 5, so one more"She is working it out from a fact she ownsGood. Keep going, speed comes later
Counts up out loud, or on fingers, one at a timeNot automatic yetGo back to the ten frame in question 2
Always

Do not say "stop using your fingers"

Banning fingers does not remove the counting, it hides it. She will count silently instead, which is slower and you can no longer see it happening.

Say this instead: "Show me on the ten frame." Or: "What goes with 7 to fill the frame?" The frame gives her something to see, and seeing is what replaces counting.
