Unit 11 // For the parent // Two minutes

Before the sheet

Everything here was taught in an earlier unit. That is the point: recall fades unless it is asked for again. Run this before the session, out loud, with no pencil. If an answer has gone, say it yourself and move on, then look at that unit again this week.

00:00

Say the rules once, then start

“A few quick questions from before. If you do not know one, say pass. Pass costs nothing.” Keep it moving. Speed is not the goal, but hesitation tells you where to go back to.

1:30 to 2:00

Round 2, how did you know?

Pick one the student answered quickly and ask how they knew. This is the whole diagnostic. “I just knew it” and “I counted” are the same answer with very different futures.
