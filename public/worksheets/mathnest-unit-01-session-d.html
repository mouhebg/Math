Cross the ten

What you need: About 20 small identical things: dried beans, nickels, buttons, or pasta. No objects? Draw dots in the squares and rub them out as they move.

When the answer goes past ten, make the ten first. Then say it as ten and something. Dix et cinq.

Move on when

The student says the total, not the leftover. "Ten and five, fifteen." If the student answers a bridging question with the small number alone, the ten is still being dropped, so go back to question 1.

For the parent

Ask the last question so the ten comes last, never the leftover. Not "and what is left from the seven?" but "the frame is full, so that is a ten, and how many are outside?" Then have the student say the whole thing: ten and five, fifteen.

Keep the objects you modelled with, the beans or coins, on the table until the student stops needing them. A child who has to remember the ten will drop it. A child who can see the ten will not.
