The rainbow of ten.

Each arc joins two numbers that make 10. Write the partner at the other end.

One pair has no arc, because it joins a number to itself. Which one? 5 and 5.

Move on when

Donia gives the partner of any number to 10 straight away, without counting up and without looking at a frame. A short pause is fine. Counting on fingers means repeat this session.
