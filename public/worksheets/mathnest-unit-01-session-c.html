Move on when

The student names the double before answering, without being asked. "Seven and seven is fourteen, so seven and eight is fifteen." A pause while the student finds the double is fine. Counting up one by one means repeat this session.

For the parent

Near doubles are the shortest route for any pair one apart: 6 + 7, 7 + 8, 8 + 9. Reach for this before making ten, because it holds only one fact in the head instead of three. Session D covers the pairs that are further apart.
